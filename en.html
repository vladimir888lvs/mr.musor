Frequently asked questions

I already pay for trash removal in my utility bill. Why Mr. Musor?

Your utility bill pays for emptying the container in your yard. Mr. Musor carries your trash from your door to that container, so you don’t have to go out yourself.

What if I forget to put my bag out?

No problem — put it out on the next pickup day. The courier goes floor by floor on schedule.

Why only Mr. Musor bags?

Our branded bags and the door sticker let the courier spot subscriber apartments instantly and never touch anyone else’s trash. Bags are included: strong, leak-proof, scented, with drawstring handles.

How do I cancel?

Turn off autopay in Kaspi and message us on WhatsApp. We keep serving you until the end of the paid month.

Do you serve my building?

We launch complex by complex. Sign up — once enough neighbors in your building join, we’ll start and let you know.
